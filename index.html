Ignacio Cajiao Hernandez

Hi, my name is Ignacio. I'm a game designer with hands on experience across multiple areas of development, including 3D modeling, coding, systems design, UI/UX, cinematography and level design. Exploring different parts of the process has helped me understand how each discipline shapes the player experience.

I’m currently focusing on level design, game design, and UX, areas where I enjoy shaping player flow, readability, and moment to moment interaction. I like breaking down how players think, how they navigate spaces, and how design choices guide their decisions and experience. I’m working toward roles that blend creativity, player psychology, and clear communication.
